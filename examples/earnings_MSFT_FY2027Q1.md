## What drove the quarter (MD&A)

- Revenue rose 18.8% to $92.28B from $77.67B, led by the Agents and Infra segment, where revenue increased $15.1 billion or 25% [^9][^10].
- Azure revenue increased $10.8 billion or 48%, which the filing attributes to demand for services across the platform with continued growth across all workloads [^10].
- Microsoft 365 commercial cloud revenue grew 17% on higher revenue per user, reflecting upsell to premium seat offerings (Microsoft 365 Copilot, E5, E7) and increased GitHub Copilot usage [^10].
- In Devices and Consumer, Windows OEM and devices revenue decreased $1.0 billion or 22%, after prior-year Windows 10 end-of-support demand, OEM and channel inventory drawdowns, and weaker PC demand [^11].
- Gross margin was 67.8% against 69.0% a year earlier [^12], but operating expenses grew only 6% [^10], so operating income rose 21.0% to $45.92B [^13]. Diluted EPS rose 32.3% to $4.92 [^14].

## What changed in risk factors

24 paragraph(s) in the 10-Q's Part II, Item 1A have no close match in the latest 10-K's Item 1A; the summary covers the first 8.

- The 10-Q adds that AI agents and models with access to internal infrastructure, sensitive data, development environments or operational tools may present risks like highly privileged users, and may take unintended actions, be manipulated by malicious inputs, or be compromised. [^15]
- It states that AI and cybersecurity risks "increasingly overlap and amplify one another", that AI agents' actions can be hard to predict, attribute, contain or audit, and that interactions among agents may amplify malicious behaviors. [^16]
- Threat-actor language is expanded to cite AI-assisted social engineering, nation-state attacks that have increased partly because of Microsoft's transparency about cyberattacks, and incidents caused by failures to patch end-of-life equipment. [^17][^18]
- It adds supply-chain compromise risk (malware via software updates, suppliers or open-source code) and says accelerating AI-driven vulnerability discovery requires faster development and patch deployment. [^19][^20]
